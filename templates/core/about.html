Who We Are

Slow Travel Rooted in Reverence for the Land

Wanderlust was born out of an undeniable intuition: standard tourism turns sacred places into quick photo stops, exhausting travelers and disconnecting them from culture. We set out to build an intimate travel atelier that listens deeply and crafts quiet, restorative journeys.

We maintain direct partnerships with indigenous family estates, organic vineyard custodians, and marine biologists. From secluded timber ryokans in Kyoto to private tented sanctuaries across the Serengeti, every passage is shaped with reverence, stillness, and impeccable care.

Chronicle

Historical Milestones

2012
Inception in Zurich & Kyoto

Wanderlust was established with one shared conviction: travel should be an art form that honors native communities rather than a hurried commercial tour.

2016
The Native Sanctuary Network

Partnered directly with private forest reserves, organic olive groves, and architectural ryokans across 18 countries.

2020
Carbon-Negative Pledge

Introduced rigorous regenerative sustainability benchmarks. Began contributing 5% of all bookings to local wildlife corridors and watershed preservation.

2024
Global Boutique Honor

Named the world's most trusted slow travel agency by the Sustainable Luxury Travel Circle, maintaining 99% 5-star traveler satisfaction.
