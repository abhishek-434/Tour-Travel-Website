Terms of Passage & Service

Last Updated: Current Operating Season

1. Scope of Service

Wanderlust designs custom, private journeys and acts as the liaison curator between travelers and boutique lodgings, native guides, and private transit charters.

2. Reservations & Confirmations

All itineraries are subject to seasonal availability. A reservation is officially recorded upon verification of payment, following which official travel vouchers and arrival coordinates are delivered.

3. Mindful Travel & Environmental Care

Wanderlust travelers agree to abide by the environmental and heritage protection guidelines established at each sanctuary destination, respecting sacred tribal lands, wildlife boundaries, and local customs.

4. Force Majeure & Flexible Rebooking

In the rare event of severe weather conditions, volcanic activity, or aviation stoppages beyond reasonable control, our concierge facilitates complimentary rescheduling or travel credits wherever viable.
