Privacy & Data Stewardship

Last Updated: Current Operating Season

At Wanderlust, your privacy is treated with the same mindful respect as the remote sanctuaries we guide you through. This charter clarifies how your travel records and personal credentials are safeguarded.

1. Information Entrusted to Us

When reserving itineraries or creating an account, we collect necessary guest particulars:

  • Traveler identity: Full legal passport names, ages, dietary specifications.
  • Direct liaison contacts: Verified email address, phone numbers, and residence.
  • Passage preferences: Historic destinations, special celebrations, and accessibility requirements.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your details are strictly used to orchestrate your private journey:

  • Confirming private retreat and wilderness lodge accommodations.
  • Coordinating regional aviation, fast catamarans, and chauffeured transit.
  • Delivering timely concierge dispatches, weather advisories, and voucher receipts.
  • Zero sale of information: We never sell or exchange traveler data with external marketing brokers.

3. Modern Encryption & Safeguards

All transaction transmissions are secured with modern 256-bit TLS protocols. Passport records are strictly siloed and accessible only to your assigned travel designer.
